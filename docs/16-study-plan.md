# 16 — Study Plan (4 / 8 / 12 Weeks)

> Calendars over the syllabus in [01](./01-fullstack-syllabus.md). **12-week ≈ 480 h** at 5.5–6 h/day. **8-week ≈ 300–340 h** (all High + most-asked Medium). **4-week ≈ 140–170 h** (core High). Parallel from day 1: 1 DSA ([13](./13-coding-round-practice.md)), 5 puzzles while in [02](./02-javascript.md)/[14](./14-js-logical-interview-questions.md), 1 STAR/week ([15](./15-other-interview-questions.md)). JD re-weights; DSA/STAR never get stolen. Do not launch billed AWS or the user's Postgres/Redis to "complete" a week.
